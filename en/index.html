FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

How long does a website take?

Most sites are ready in 2–3 weeks, depending on size and how quickly we receive your content.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once paid, the site and its code are yours. No lock‑in.

Do you use templates?

No. Every site is designed and coded from scratch for your business, so it's faster and unique.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo (if you have one), texts about your business, and photos. If you don't have them, we can help.

How do I pay?

50% when we start, 50% at launch. Bank transfer or card.

What if I don't pay for monthly care?

Your site stays online. You only pay in the months you need changes.
